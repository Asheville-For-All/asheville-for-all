August Meetup

August 28, 2025
Hi-Wire Brewing RAD Beer Garden, 284 Lyman St., Asheville NC 28801

Come join and meet your fellow pro-housing Ashevilleans!

Any and all are welcome. We’ll be gathering at the Hi-Wire Brewing Beer Garden, from 6:00pm-8:00pm, on Thursday, August 28th. Nothing too formal, just a chance to hang out, meet people, and talk about housing in Asheville.

The Hi-Wire Beer Garden is family-friendly, and offers non-alcoholic options. There is usually a food truck parked outside. (You may also bring outside food.)

How will you find our table? Look for the small “table topper” that says “Asheville For All”!

If you fill out the RSVP form below to let us know that you’re planning to come, then we can send you an email reminder the day before. And we can better keep you informed on any last minute details too.
