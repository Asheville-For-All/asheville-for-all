Let City Council Know That Complete Streets Is Affordability Policy

September 29, 2023

Asheville City Council approved the resolution to move forward with the “complete streets” College Patton Bike Lane Project on October 10th, 2023.

Please sign the petition below by Thursday, October 5th. We will submit the list of signatures in a letter to City Council on the following day.

On October 10th, Asheville City Council will be voting on a resolution regarding a “Complete Streets” plan for College St. and Patton Ave. downtown.

Asheville For All believes that moving away from automobile-exclusive street design and towards those that welcome pedestrians and bicyclists is a matter of affordability and inclusivity. If we’re going to have housing abundance, and a variety of housing options for ALL kinds of Ashevilleans and their families, we need to embrace a variety of transportation modes, especially modes that are less costly, less polluting, and more space-efficient than automobiles.

Please Take the Following Steps to Let City Council Know That You Support Complete Streets in Asheville

  1. Read Asheville For All’s full statement on the link between affordability and “complete streets” here.

  2. Sign your name in support of the statement. Sign the petition below by Thursday, October 5th. We will submit the list of signatures in a letter to City Council on the following day.

Want to learn more? The city has a webpage with all the details as well as answers to frequently asked questions about the planned bicycle and pedestrian improvements downtown here.
