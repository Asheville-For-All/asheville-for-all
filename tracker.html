Disclaimer

  • This page shows votes related to municipal land use policy and housing policy for the selected duration of time. Asheville For All makes no claims for the comprehensiveness of the votes listed on this page.

  • We take care to note that elected officials may have all kinds of reasons for voting against what in our estimation is a “pro-housing” motion. How valid such decisions are cannot always be easily captured in a resource such as this. Where possible, we try to provide links to media coverage of each vote in order that you may obtain context.

How to Navigate

The web app's main panel shows Asheville City Council Meeting agenda items that pertained to housing policy or related land use policy, in reverse chronology. You can click on any item to see more details.

The panel at the bottom of screen shows Asheville's current sitting councilors. The councilors are presented in an order that is based on their voting records for the items listed in the above panel. You can click on any councilor to see more information.

Choose the settings menu in the top menu to adjust how many years the app goes back in its calculations.

Note: Choosing a longer duration for looking back may result in "apples to oranges" comparisons. Consider a case, for example, where a new councilor is presented alongside one whose term ended several years ago.

Comparison Tool

This app's most useful aspect may be its "comparison tool." Choose the blue icon in the top menu to compare different councilors.
